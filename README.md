This project explores how brand tribalism influences brand love among consumers. It is based on primary research and statistical analysis.

## Overview
The study examines how emotional connection, community belonging, and shared identity around brands affect consumer behavior and loyalty.

## Methodology
- Data collected using structured questionnaires
- Sample size: 154 respondents
- Analysis techniques: Correlation and Regression

## Key Findings
- Brand tribalism has a positive impact on brand love
- Consumers feel more attached when they identify with a brand community
- Emotional and social connections increase loyalty
